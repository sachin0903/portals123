Client Service Agreement

Between Acme Advisers Ltd ("the Firm") and Margaret Chen ("the Client")

1. Scope of Service

The Firm will provide ongoing personal financial advice, including annual suitability assessments, portfolio reviews, and access to your named adviser. Ad-hoc guidance is included within the annual service fee unless otherwise agreed in writing.

2. Fees and Charges

Fees are set out in your current Fee Agreement. You will receive written notice of any changes to the fee schedule no less than 30 days before they take effect. You may terminate the service relationship at any time by providing written notice.

3. Communication

Your adviser will contact you at least once per year for a formal review. Between reviews, you may reach your adviser by secure message, telephone, or email during business hours (Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm).

4. Your Responsibilities

You agree to inform the Firm promptly of any material changes to your circumstances, financial position, or objectives that may affect the suitability of advice provided.

5. Conflicts of Interest & Governing Law

The Firm maintains a written Conflicts of Interest Policy. These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales, without prejudice to your right to refer a complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service.
