Recommendations for 2026
  • 01 Increase your ISA contribution to £20,000 for the 2026/27 tax year to maximise tax-efficient growth ahead of your drawdown phase. Your current contribution of £15,000 leaves £5,000 of allowance unused.
  • 02 Rebalance the global equity weighting from 46% to 42%, redirecting £19,500 into the fixed income allocation. This moves the portfolio closer to target as markets approach historically elevated valuations.
  • 03 Review the cash holding in light of the current interest rate environment. We suggest switching £15,000 from the money market fund into a 12-month fixed-rate cash ISA at 4.65% AER, subject to your liquidity needs.
  • 04 Consider a nominated beneficiary review for your pension plan, particularly in light of the changes announced in the Autumn 2025 Budget regarding inherited pension taxation from 2027.
