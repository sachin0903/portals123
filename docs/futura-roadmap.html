Governance & assurance

Weekly working software. Every week delivers a demonstrable increment against the agreed baseline, so progress is visible rather than back-loaded to a single release. A weekly update (from the Jira board, with burn-down/velocity) reports done / next / risks; a monthly Steering Committee confirms alignment. A real-client pilot (5–10 clients) proves the end-to-end journey before go-live, followed by an operational readiness review. Later phases (servicing, full lifecycle and further enhancements) are captured in a day-2/3 backlog and sequenced after Phase 1.
