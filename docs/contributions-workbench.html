How detection works: contributions are surfaced from the daily SEI credit feed — the SWP/Boxi transaction report of all credits into the ML SEI bank accounts in the last 24h — joined to the canonical book. Each becomes a pensions.contributions row at identified; Ops classifies and confirms it, the API records it to ML (cam_createpensioncontribution), and the row advances through the relief-at-source lifecycle to reconciled. Regular contributions arrive by standing order to SEI 40-05-30 / 13695581.

Relief at Source — reclaim & relief monitor

Personal contributions are paid net of 20% basic-rate tax; the scheme reclaims the relief from HMRC. Morgan Lloyd aggregates the month's personal contributions and reclaims the basic-rate relief from HMRC; once received, ML remits a bulk payment + a per-client breakdown to SEI. Graphene raises one bulk Additional Cash Receipt (ACR) on SEI and sends the breakdown spreadsheet; SEI's loader credits the individual member accounts. This page tracks each monthly batch and the relief that is still in transit.

Manual touchpoint (go-live): the contributions integration ships automated; the only residual manual step is the bulk-ACR reclaim — Ops raises one ACR on SEI and uploads the breakdown. As volume grows this becomes upload-driven, then a fully automated ACR + breakdown ingest.

Relief in transit — expected vs received (per member)

The forecast relief for each recorded personal contribution, netted against what has actually landed from HMRC. In-transit relief is what reconciliation nets out so it doesn't raise a false cash break.

Three-way reconciliation — contributions

Daily check of the Graphene book vs SEI custody vs the Morgan Lloyd scheme across contribution cash. Tolerances and as-of alignment apply; in-flight items (RAS relief in transit, contributions recorded to ML but not yet credited) are netted so the control doesn't cry wolf. Unresolved differences become platform.reconciliation_breaks.

Status mappings

How one contribution's status reads across the three systems. The workbench tracks the canonical pensions.contribution_status enum; each value corresponds to a Morgan Lloyd contribution status and to the SEI transaction status of two distinct cash legs — the member's initial contribution payment and the tax-relief payment. A contribution and its relief are posted to SEI as separate cash receipts, so each carries its own SEI activity status.

Provenance — read before using. The workbench column is authoritative (the DB enum). The SEI columns are verbatim from the SEI OpenAPI specs. The Morgan Lloyd contribution status option-set is not yet documented: only "Received" is evidenced (a cam_getreceivedcontributions response). Values tagged TBC are inferred from the workflow + the transfers analogue and must be confirmed against the live ML sandbox. The ML status field name (cam_status vs Dataverse statuscode/statecode) is likewise TBC.

The member's funds reach SEI by bank transfer / standing order (40-05-30 / 13695581). SEI records the incoming credit as a cash-receipt activity; by the time it surfaces in the daily feed it is normally already posted. Where funds land in a collection account and Graphene allocates them to the member, the cash-receipt requestStatus path applies. One contribution, three clocks. The workbench status is the single source of truth Ops works from; it is derived from the ML contribution status (advanced by polling cam_updatecontributionpension_status hourly) and the two SEI cash-receipt activity statuses (read via RetrieveActivityState / RetrieveTransactionsForClientAccount). Three-way reconciliation is exactly the check that these three clocks agree once in-transit relief is netted out; a disagreement past tolerance becomes a platform.reconciliation_breaks row and the contribution drops to manual_intervention.

Relief at source

British SIPP, relief-at-source only — no net-pay/salary-sacrifice arrangements. The workbench does not run an eligibility check.

  • Employer contributions are paid gross. No tax relief is reclaimed — the employer takes the corporation-tax deduction. Captured as Gross Single / Gross Regular.
  • Personal contributions are made net of basic-rate tax relief; the scheme claims the relief at basic rate (20%) from HMRC. Captured as Net Single / Net Regular. Gross = net ÷ 0.80; relief = gross − net.

Prototype scope. Mock data, no live API calls — actions log the operation they would invoke. Terminology is pinned to the canonical schema (pensions.contributions, pensions.contribution_status, platform.transaction_expectations, platform.reconciliation_breaks), the Morgan Lloyd cam_* OData surface (contributiontype on the ML side is Member/Employer; this app stores personal/employer), and the SEI cash-receipt APIs. RAS/recon run as scheduled jobs (ml_contributions_poll hourly, ml_recon daily). Custodian note: Seccl (the alternative custodian) has no first-class relief-at-source or net/gross fields — contributions are plain cash movements — so the RAS lifecycle is owned by this app's pensions.contributions schema regardless of custodian.
